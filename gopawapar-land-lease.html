Gopawapar Village sits on the wide Pradhan Mantri Rojgar Yojna Road, between Karuana Chauraha and Paiana Village Chauraha — situated on the Ram Janki Marg, a four-lane National Highway going directly towards Bihar. The village lies on the link road between Karuana Chauraha and Paiana Chauraha.

Note on mapping: In digital mapping records the village appears as "Gopalapar". The correct name as given in Revenue Records (Khatauni & Kharsa) of the UP Government is Revenue Village — GOPAWAPAR.
